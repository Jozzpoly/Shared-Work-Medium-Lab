## What this does and does not prove

### Supports

> Medium can preserve one real Browser-observed work object from another project through the generic passive substrate without first standardizing what that object “is”.

It also supports a stronger architectural lesson:

> explicit declared roles should outrank accidental file-shape inference.

### Does not support

- ecological discovery;
- usefulness to another project;
- automatic import of ChatGPT conversations;
- a final provenance model;
- permission to publish arbitrary private conversation content;
- a global ontology for research findings;
- search/recommendation/feed;
- active execution.

## New frontier

The next high-value question is no longer object representation.

It is whether a real shared thing can acquire a **quiet, truthful doorway** into another workstream without Browser/Owner pre-deciding its relevance.

That pressure should be attacked independently before adding search or recommendation.

## Why this exists

Medium currently has several different kinds of "current":

1. **accepted public baseline** — what `main` has actually accepted;
2. **active draft frontier** — work living in open PRs/branches that may be newer but is not accepted baseline;
3. **live/private research** — conversation/Library work that may be newer still and is not automatically public or repo authority.

Those must not be collapsed into one truth plane.

At the time this specimen was created:

- public accepted baseline: `main@d190d17db8edb5e6e41f8e5777fe2dc0894280d1`;
- `docs/RESEARCH_STATE.md` says its last material update is 2026-10-04;
- draft PR #8 contains the newer source-bound capability-expedition evidence;
- draft PRs #5/#6 contain Quiet Presence / exchange-window evidence;
- draft PR #4 contains the MP-1 controlled apparatus;
- newer live research may exist outside the public repository and must not be invented or treated as public authority.

The purpose of this branch is only to test whether **explicit topology** makes recovery safer than telling a fresh agent that one large document is simply "the current live state".

### Working consequence

The stronger project-local pattern currently looks closer to:

> **stable route, live meaning**

with:
- stable/thin front doors that mostly route;
- volatile execution truth kept in the smallest representation that can stay truthful;
- immutable result/evidence artifacts kept separate;
- presentation surfaces free to evolve as long as their authority routing remains explicit.

This remains donor evidence, not a required Medium schema.

## ReflexBrain now has two distinct local media

A later source-qualified follow-up on
[`ReflexBrain-Lab@287901ff3053465e4ffc23b5a297b2c791771135`](https://github.com/Jozzpoly/ReflexBrain-Lab/commit/287901ff3053465e4ffc23b5a297b2c791771135)
shows that the earlier "local medium" interpretation was still underspecified.

ReflexBrain currently has **two different local medium functions**:

1. **orientation / authority medium**
   - thin project/front-door routing;
   - one mutable execution-state authority;
   - immutable run/result evidence;
   - historical probes and branches kept as evidence rather than current truth.

2. **project-native interactive scientific medium**
   - explicitly defined in `REFLEXBRAIN_INTERACTIVE_MEDIUM_CAMPAIGNS_2026-10-07.md`;
   - Habitat/Field, Evidence Atlas, Chronicle/Continuity, Workbench/Forks and future Organism Observatory;
   - intended to let Owner enter the actual specimen, perturb it, observe continuation, preserve/fork interesting states and turn surprise into science;
   - explicitly forbidden from becoming actor knowledge, scientific truth, or a substitute for organism qualification.

The live Owner Field Lab is currently marked **UNQUALIFIED** and already carries known long-run/process defects rather than being polished into a false PASS.

**Medium consequence:** Shared Work Medium should not treat a project-native scientific instrument as state to centralize. A more plausible role is to help another participant discover that such an instrument exists, enter through its declared local authority/evidence topology, and preserve its scope/freshness/nonclaims during transfer.

This is still a hypothesis. If ordinary project-native front doors and search already provide reliable cross-project discovery and transfer, Shared Work Medium may have little additional role.

## Evaluation question

For a fresh agent with a real task:

> Does this explicit topology help it reach the right authority faster, while preserving the distinction between accepted baseline, draft evidence and volatile live state?

A useful outcome may be:

- use accepted baseline;
- inspect one draft PR;
- inspect live source;
- decide nothing newer is relevant;
- or correctly report that the public repo is insufficient for the requested live claim.

The goal is not maximal retrieval.

## Falsifiers

Reject or revise this pattern if:

- fresh agents still treat draft work as accepted truth;
- the extra routing layer increases reading burden without changing decisions;
- it becomes another stale handoff that must be manually maintained everywhere;
- project-local entrypoints alone already solve the problem;
- the topology pressures all projects into the same representation.

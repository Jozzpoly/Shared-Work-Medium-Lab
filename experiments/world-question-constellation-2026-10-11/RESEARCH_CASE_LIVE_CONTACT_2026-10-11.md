## What might transfer — and why it probably fails if copied naively

**Hypothesis H:** A lawful actor-private relation between intended morph actuation and recent body-local contact can sometimes help distinguish **physically constrained expansion** from counterproductive forced expansion, *without* reading omniscient host clearance or regret. This is an **unproven cross-world question**, not a claim that F3A's Q already works for hinges or collider resizing.

**Primary falsifier:** **before first physical contact**, a folded actor may have identical private touch + motor demand in two worlds with different upcoming gate clearance. Touch alone cannot tell those worlds apart. Likewise, simultaneous contact from multiple bodies can cancel signed components, and hinges may remain mechanically constrained without a single directionally useful normal. A signal that only detects severe collision *after* the cost has been incurred may be useless for anticipatory action.

**Fair controls:** never compare the actor only to the naive catastrophic collider injection. Compare against (A) always hold, (B) always expand, (C) lawful cheap current-touch threshold, (D) cheap collision-aware shape-limit / local clearance baseline, (E) F3A-inspired action-relative relation *without retuning on held-out test*. Use the **same changing material world** and action costs. Host regret is only a test evaluator, never agent-private input.

**Possible bounded experiment (not implemented):**
1. Define an actor-owned view of candidate shape actuation, collision impulses/timing and allowed local sight/touch; freeze its permitted observations before generating outcomes.
2. Distinguish *pre-contact approach*, *wall-obstructed expansion*, *post-clearance expansion*, *off-axis simultaneous contact* and *unseen wall change after last lawful sensor frame*.
3. Record both choices' physical aftermath in the same initial world and cost measure, compare against all cheap controls, and explicitly score failure to detect aliased cases.
4. Permit a third option of **acquire a new lawful observation** before attempting expansion; compare its delay/opportunity cost against conservative hold.
5. If a cheap control matches the proposed sensor across varied test families, or prior private states are causally indistinguishable, **stop and report negative**. Do not merge into Combat or ReflexBrain by conceptual resemblance.
